Questions

Is RM50 a one-time payment?

RM50 gives you 30 days. Nothing renews by itself. To continue, pay again for the next 30 days and the same code is extended. You can also pay 2 to 12 months at once at the same rate, for example 3 months RM150 or 12 months RM600.

Premium or VIP: which one do I need?

Premium shows everything you need to judge a unit: address, market value, bank and price history. VIP adds 12 months of auction results and the investor score, for people who buy to invest. You can start with Premium and upgrade later.

How fast do I get my code?

Usually the same day, once the payment is confirmed. It comes on WhatsApp with the link.

Can I try before paying?

Yes. The free preview shows every unit with type, area, size, price and auction date. Premium unlocks the details on the same list.

Where does the information come from?

From the public auction notices (proclamations of sale) of the banks, collected and checked every day. Always confirm the details with the auctioneer before you bid.

Can I share my code?

The code is personal, for your own phone and family. A code that is passed around can be switched off.

Who is behind the list?

Zulkifli. Questions before you pay? WhatsApp 013-9402396.

The list is a reference to help you find auction properties. It is not legal or financial advice, and Zulkifli is not the auctioneer or the bank. Auction dates, reserve prices and conditions can change; always check the proclamation of sale and the auctioneer's announcement before bidding.
