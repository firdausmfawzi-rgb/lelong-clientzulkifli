Questions

Is RM50 a one-time payment?

RM50 gives you 30 days of access. Nothing renews by itself. If you want to continue, you pay again for the next 30 days, and the same code is extended. You can also pay 2 to 12 months at once at the same rate, for example 3 months RM150 or 12 months RM600.

What do I get after paying?

A personal access code, sent on WhatsApp. Enter it once on the Premium site and the phone remembers it.

Where does the information come from?

From the public auction notices (proclamations of sale) of the banks, collected and checked every day. Always confirm the details with the auctioneer before you bid.

Can I share my code?

The code is personal, for your own phone and family. A code that is passed around can be switched off.
